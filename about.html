About Lesotho

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. It is known as the Kingdom in the Sky.

Our Culture

Basotho culture is known for its traditional blankets, the mokorotlo hat, music, crafts and hospitality.

Why Visit Lesotho?

  • Beautiful mountain
  • Waterfalls and dams
  • Traditional Basotho culture
  • Hiking
  • Peaceful rural landscapes
Lesotho offers visitors a special combination of nature, adventure and culture.
